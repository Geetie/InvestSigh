[L1] NVIDIA Announces Financial Results for Second Quarter Fiscal 2027
[L2] Revenue of $96.2 billion, up 106% from a year ago
[L3] Data Center revenue of $89.0 billion, up 117% from a year ago

[L5] NVIDIA (NASDAQ: NVDA) today reported revenue for the second quarter ended July 26, 2026,
     of $96.2 billion, up 18% from the previous quarter and up 106% from a year ago.
     For the quarter, GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins were both 75.0%.
     GAAP and non-GAAP earnings per diluted share were $2.46 and $2.22, respectively.

[L7] "AI has reached its inflection point. It's doing useful work. Its tokens are productive and
     profitable. Now, compute is revenue," said Jensen Huang, founder and CEO of NVIDIA.
     "And demand is accelerating. This time last year, one lab alone was driving the buildout;
     today, we have a golden age of new AI labs and startups, multiple frontier labs scaling in
     parallel, a thriving open-model ecosystem and physical AI coming online — with strong
     momentum across the U.S. and around the world. The AI infrastructure buildout is at full
     steam. Vera Rubin, now in full production, was built to power exactly this moment."

[L11] During the second quarter of fiscal 2027, NVIDIA returned approximately $26.0 billion to
      shareholders in the form of shares repurchased and cash dividends. As of the end of the
      second quarter, the company had approximately $99.0 billion remaining under its share
      repurchase authorization.

[L13] NVIDIA will pay its next quarterly cash dividend of $0.25 per share on October 1, 2026,
      to all shareholders of record on September 10, 2026.

[L24] Outlook
[L25] NVIDIA's outlook for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 is as follows:
[L26] - Revenue is expected to be $108.0 billion, plus or minus 2%.
       NVIDIA is not assuming any Data Center compute revenue from China in its outlook.
[L28] - GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins are expected to be 74.0%, plus or minus 50 basis points.
[L29] - GAAP and non-GAAP operating expenses are expected to be approximately $9.2 billion and
       $9.0 billion, respectively.
[L31] For the full year fiscal 2027, NVIDIA expects GAAP and non-GAAP tax rates to be between
      16.0% and 18.0%, excluding any discrete items and material changes to NVIDIA's tax environment.

[L33] Highlights
[L34] Data Center
[L35] Second-quarter revenue was $89.0 billion, up 18% from the previous quarter and up 117% from
      a year ago.
[L36] Announced the NVIDIA Vera Rubin platform is ramping into full production with racks running
      at partners including CoreWeave, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, Oracle Cloud Infrastructure
      and Nebius.
[L37] Revealed that NVIDIA Spectrum-6 switch systems — supporting both pluggable and co-packaged
      optics as part of the NVIDIA Vera Rubin platform — are arriving across the world's gigascale
      AI factories.
[L38] Unveiled NVIDIA Vera, the first CPU built for AI agents, with broad adoption planned across
      the world's leading technology providers.

[L40] Edge Computing
[L41] Second-quarter Edge Computing revenue was $7.2 billion, up 13% from the previous quarter and
      up 27% from a year ago.

[L43] Announced strategic partnerships with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield,
      Goldman Sachs and KKR to create independent compute financing platforms, to mobilize more
      than $500 billion of third-party capital for AI infrastructure buildout, subject to
      definitive agreements.
